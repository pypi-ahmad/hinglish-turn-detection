Lesson 1

Silence does not mean finished

A voice assistant first notices that speech stopped. It still has to decide whether the speaker completed a thought or paused inside one.

Complete, label 1

"Haan, bas." The assistant may answer.

Incomplete, label 0

"Haan... ek second..." The assistant should wait.

VAD and turn detection answer different questions

Voice activity detection asks whether speech is present. Turn detection asks why speech stopped. Timing alone cannot answer that. Fillers, syntax, rhythm, and intonation can all change the meaning of the same pause.

The expensive error

A false complete occurs when an incomplete turn is predicted complete. The assistant interrupts. A false incomplete usually adds delay. Both matter, but they do not have equal product cost.

FCR = false completes / all actually incomplete examples

Why Hinglish is difficult

People switch between Hindi and English within a thought. Words such as "matlab", "actually", "toh", "yaar", and "haan" can hold the floor, but they can also end a reply. A model that treats every filler as unfinished will fail on short complete answers.
