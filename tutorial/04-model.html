Lesson 4

A speech encoder with one small decision head

Whisper Tiny supplies multilingual acoustic features. The project discards its text decoder because turn detection needs one probability, not a transcript.

audio, mono 16 kHz, final 8 s
  to log-mel spectrogram
  to Whisper Tiny encoder
  to 400 vectors, each 384 values
  to pooling
  to classifier: 384, 256, 64, 1
  to P(turn complete)

Pooling is the argument

Mean pooling gives every valid frame equal weight. Attention pooling learns where to look. Last-frame pooling uses the final encoder representation. The experiments test all three instead of assuming attention must win.

The selected E4 model uses last-frame pooling. It has 7,901,569 parameters and a 30.14 MiB FP32 parameter file.

Probability is not a decision

Sigmoid turns the logit into a number between 0 and 1. A threshold then converts that probability into complete or incomplete. Historical ablations used 0.5. The production checkpoint stores a validation-selected threshold of 0.5777403.

Say this clearly

Changing the threshold does not retrain the model. It moves the operating point between interruptions and delayed responses.

Why not default to audio plus text?

The optional model adds transcript embeddings, but live transcription costs time. Its small F1 gain did not compensate for worse false-complete rate and much slower end-to-end inference.
