Lesson 5

Training teaches a score. Validation chooses its use.

The loop learns logits with binary cross-entropy, then measures whether those logits are useful at a safe operating point.

One training step

  1. Load and possibly augment a waveform.
  2. Left-pad it and extract Whisper features.
  3. Run encoder, pooling, and classifier.
  4. Compare logits with labels using BCE with logits.
  5. Backpropagate, clip gradients, update with AdamW, then step the cosine schedule.

Mixed precision reduces memory use. Gradient accumulation creates a larger effective batch without requiring it to fit at once.

Read metrics as a set

MetricQuestion
AccuracyHow often is either class correct?
PrecisionWhen model says complete, how often is it right?
RecallHow many truly complete turns does it release?
F1How well do precision and recall balance?
AUCHow well do scores rank classes across thresholds?
FCRHow often does it interrupt incomplete turns?

Safety calibration

For each run, validation probabilities are searched for the best F1 while requiring FCR at most 10 percent and recall at least 85 percent. The chosen threshold and confusion counts are stored beside the weights.

A guardrail can fail after selection

The final held-out FCR stayed below 10 percent, but recall reached 83.26 percent, below the desired 85 percent. That is evidence of distribution shift or uncertainty, not permission to retune on test.
