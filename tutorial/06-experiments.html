Lesson 6

Use ablations to isolate each choice

Each ablation changes a controlled part of the system. The result should tell us something about model behavior, even when it is worse.

What the first study found

  • Full augmentation raised F1 by 0.27 points but worsened FCR by 1.03 points.
  • Attention beat mean pooling by 1.52 F1 points.
  • Last-frame pooling had the best AUC and hard-Hinglish proxy F1.
  • A fully frozen encoder lost 14.60 F1 points.
  • Partial freezing kept nearly all the F1 with 4.45 million trainable parameters.
  • Audio plus text gained 0.25 F1 points, but live inference was 27.6 times slower and FCR was worse.

Because these results come from one seed, they suggest directions rather than prove that one setup is always better.

Finalist study

E1, E4, and E8 were then trained for six epochs with seeds 42, 43, and 44. E4 had the best median constrained validation F1 at 89.82 percent. Seed 44 was the median E4 run, so its checkpoint was selected.

What changed the result

Data policy affected the safety operating point as much as model design did. A higher aggregate F1 did not necessarily mean fewer interruptions.
