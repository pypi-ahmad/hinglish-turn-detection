Lesson 3

Every augmentation needs a reason

Each transform addresses a known weakness. Transforms run only during training, so validation and test audio stay unchanged.

TransformPurpose
100 to 800 ms silencePrevent the model from assuming that silence always means complete
Hinglish filler insertionExpose the model to words such as "matlab" and "ek second"
Speed 0.9 to 1.1xReduce dependence on speaking rate
Pitch within 2 semitonesAdd speaker variation without badly changing prosody
Volume within 6 dBKeep microphone gain from becoming a cue
Noise at 5 to 20 dB SNRTest audio outside clean recording conditions

Why both labels receive fillers

A filler often signals that the speaker will continue, but not always. Adding fillers only to incomplete clips would teach the model that the synthetic filler voice means label 0. Instead, the pipeline inserts fillers inside clips from both classes and keeps the original label.

Hard examples

The sampler gives more weight to short complete clips and long incomplete clips that contain fillers. This makes a simple duration rule less useful. Because these examples are not balanced, they form a false-complete training curriculum rather than a benchmark.

What synthetic audio cannot prove

TTS fillers and fallback room noise make controlled experiments possible. They do not show how the model will perform on natural Indian conversations or recordings from streets, offices, and phone calls.
