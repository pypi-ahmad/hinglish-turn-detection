Augment a failure, not a checkbox

Each transform answers a named weakness. It runs only during training, while validation and test audio remain clean.

TransformPurpose
100 to 800 ms silenceBreak the shortcut that silence always means complete

Pitch within 2 semitonesAdd speaker variation without large prosody damage
Volume within 6 dBStop microphone gain becoming a cue
Noise at 5 to 20 dB SNRTest less tidy acoustic conditions

Why both labels receive fillers

A filler often signals continuation, but not always. Injecting it only into incomplete clips would teach a synthetic shortcut: "filler voice means label 0." The project inserts fillers internally into both classes and preserves the original label.

Hard examples

The sampler gives extra weight to short complete clips and long incomplete clips with fillers. This attacks a tempting duration rule. The hard set is asymmetric, so it is a false-complete curriculum rather than a balanced benchmark.

Synthetic is coverage, not authenticity

TTS fillers and room-noise fallback make controlled experiments possible. They do not prove performance on natural Indian conversations, streets, offices, or phone channels.
